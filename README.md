GeoNexus Africa

Connecting Geological Data. Unlocking Mineral Potential.

1. Project Overview

GeoNexus is a proposed geological data integration platform designed to improve access to Nigerian geological information for mineral exploration.

The project focuses on the challenge of old, scattered, and poorly organised geological information.

2. Problem Statement

Geological maps, mineral occurrence records, historical exploration reports, and other relevant information may be distributed across different sources and formats. This can make information difficult to locate, compare, assess, and use for exploration planning.

3. Proposed Solution

GeoNexus aims to bring available geological information into a centralised, searchable platform with interactive mapping and data-quality indicators.

4. Planned Features

* Centralised geological records.
* Search and filters by mineral, location, and data source.
* Interactive GIS map of documented mineral occurrences.
* Data-source tracking and quality indicators.
* Identification of missing, incomplete, or potentially outdated information.
* AI-assisted analysis and preliminary exploration-target generation where sufficient reliable data is available.

5. Initial Scope

The first prototype will focus on accessible Nigerian geological datasets. Each record will retain its source, relevant dates, limitations, and verification status.

6. Team

* Petroleum Engineering
* Mining Engineering
* Data Analytics / Data Science
* Geology

7. Current Development Stage

Concept and initial planning. Data collection, validation, and prototype development are planned next.

8. Data Sources

Official and other suitable sources will be documented as datasets are identified and reviewed. Data availability, access permissions, and usage conditions will be checked before incorporation.

9. Disclaimer

GeoNexus is a proposed decision-support tool. Its outputs will not independently confirm the presence of an economic mineral deposit; exploration targets require appropriate geological validation.
